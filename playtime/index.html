How Long to Beat

Four numbers cover almost every way people ask this: rushing the story, a normal playthrough, one completionist run, and the full Platinum grind.

Capcom hasn't published an official chapter-by-chapter time breakdown, so these are community-tracked estimates. We cross-checked multiple independent sources and kept only the figures that agreed with each other — one estimate we found (45-50 hours for "full completion") didn't match anything else we could verify and referenced collectible categories that don't appear in our own cross-verified collectibles guide, so we left it out rather than repeat an unconfirmed number.

What actually moves the needle

  • Difficulty choice. Story and Action difficulty affect fight length more than exploration time — see our combat guide for the Parry/Deflect/Issen loop that determines how fast fights actually go.
  • Boss retries. A handful of fights (Shuten Doji, Dokyo, the Chapter 7 gauntlet, and the final boss) are built around multiple attempts even for skilled players — see our boss guides.
  • Collectible hunting habits. Genma Notes aren't marked on the map, so deliberately hunting them (rather than grabbing what you stumble across) adds real time — see our collectibles guide.
  • How much of Kyoto you explore. The game structure is mostly linear missions built around an expandable Kyoto hub, so free-roam detours are the biggest lever between the ~20h and ~25h numbers.

Pacing across the seven chapters

Our quest walkthrough covers all seven chapters and 21 missions in detail. In broad strokes, the earlier chapters (Kiyomizu-dera, Kennin-ji Whirlwind) run shorter and more tutorial-paced, while Mount Oe and the Chapter 7 finale run longer thanks to boss density — Mount Oe alone stacks three boss rematches on top of the Shuten Doji fight, and Chapter 7 opens with a rematch gauntlet before the final boss. We don't have granular per-chapter hour data confirmed across sources, so we're not going to invent exact numbers per chapter — the four tiers above are the reliable figures.

Practical takeaway: if you just want the credits, budget ~20 hours and skip side content. If you want the full experience without Platinum-hunting, ~25-33 hours covers a satisfying completionist run. Only budget for the 60-70 hour range if you're actually chasing the Platinum — see our trophy guide for that full roadmap.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the main story?
Around 20 hours if you rush it.

How long to 100% the game?
About 33 hours for a single completionist playthrough, not counting the Carnage New Game+ needed for Platinum.

How long for the Platinum trophy?
Roughly 60-70 hours across two playthroughs — see our trophy guide.
