Outfits & Sword Skins Guide

Cosmetics only — none of this changes your stats. Here's every outfit, haori, and katana skin, and how to actually unlock each one.

This guide reflects what's known shortly after launch (September 4, 2026), cross-checked across multiple independent sources. The base clothing outfits and the six Lion Dog sword skins are well-corroborated with exact unlock conditions. The wider haori coat set is less settled — sources agree there are around 7 story-earnable haori plus 3 Deluxe Edition exclusives, but don't fully agree on which story mission unlocks which specific haori by name. We've marked those as unconfirmed rather than guessing at a mapping.

Katana skins

One skin every 6 Lion Dogs, all 36 rescued for the final sword

All six katana skins are earned by rescuing Lion Dogs — one for every six rescued, six skins across all 36. They're purely cosmetic reskins of Musashi's single katana (see our weapon guide for how the katana itself actually works); none of them change stats or move sets.

Haori coats

Additional haori (the coat worn over the kimono) unlock as you clear story missions — reporting agrees there are roughly seven earnable this way, on top of three Deluxe Edition exclusives. The two we can confirm by name and trigger:

  • Striped Haori — defeat the Rasho-gan boss fight and complete "Careful What You Wish For."
  • Velvet Haori — complete "What Lurks in the Shadows," the same mission tied to our mask puzzle solution.

Other missions reported to unlock a haori include "Okuni's Kabuki Dance," "The Demon on the Mount," and "In the Light of an Icy Moon," alongside a plain Gauntlet-Hiding Haori that functions as the default. Exactly which named haori each of those unlocks isn't consistent across sources yet, so we're not going to force a guess at the pairing.

Deluxe Edition exclusives

These aren't earnable through normal play — they come with the Deluxe or Premium Deluxe Edition and are claimed at a Spirit Mirror:

  • Red Armor outfit and Crimson Lotus Gauntlet
  • Hellfire, Cloudbitter, and Moonlight haori coats
  • Seven exclusive katana skins, including Bamboo and Panda, Enryuu, and White Lion
  • Alternate costumes for Izumo no Okuni and other companions

Practical takeaway: if you're not on a Deluxe Edition, don't go looking for the Red Armor or the DLC haori/skins — they're simply not obtainable in a normal playthrough. Everything else here comes from playing the story and rescuing Lion Dogs, and none of it is missable — see our trophy guide for how that safety net works.

Frequently asked questions

How do you unlock the Panda costume?
Automatically, at the credits — no other requirements.

How many katana skins are there?
Six earnable ones, one per 6 Lion Dogs rescued, all purely cosmetic.

Are any outfits Deluxe Edition exclusive?
Yes — the Red Armor and several haori and katana skins are locked to paid editions.
