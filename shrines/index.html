Shrine Locations: Spirit Mirror Guide

What the game actually calls a "shrine" is a Spirit Mirror — and it's doing more work than a simple checkpoint. Here's exactly what activating one gets you, and where they're distributed across Kyoto.

This guide reflects what's known shortly after launch (September 4, 2026), cross-checked across multiple independent community sources. We're covering distribution by area rather than a full coordinate-by-coordinate list until we've mapped every mirror ourselves.

What a Spirit Mirror actually does

One location, three functions

Interacting with a Spirit Mirror triggers an autosave and gives you access to 20 manual save slots. Fast travel between activated mirrors isn't available immediately — it unlocks a little into the story — and you can't fast travel while enemies are actively engaged with you. On the upgrade side, mirrors are where you spend Red Souls on skills (see our skill guide) and access equipment and cosmetic changes.

This lines up with the chapter structure in our quest walkthrough — mirror density roughly tracks how sprawling or vertical each area actually is, with Mount Oe (Chapter 5) leading by a wide margin.

Practical habits

  • Activate every mirror you pass, even ones slightly off your current path — the backtracking they save later is worth the short detour now.
  • Resting fully heals you, but respawns standard enemies in the surrounding area. Treat that as a downside right after clearing a room, and as a deliberate farming loop when you actually want more fights.
  • Grab Oni Vision early if you haven't (see our skill guide) — it reveals collectibles through walls and makes exploring between mirrors far less guesswork-heavy, which matters for our collectibles guide and secret areas guide too.

Practical takeaway: don't treat mirrors as purely functional waypoints — activating every one you find as you go is close to free, and Mount Oe specifically punishes anyone who skipped ahead without doing so.

Frequently asked questions

What are shrines called in-game?
Spirit Mirrors — save points, fast travel hubs, and upgrade stations combined.

Is fast travel available from the start?
No — it unlocks through early story progression, and only works between mirrors you've already activated.

Is there a downside to resting?
Yes — it respawns standard enemies in the surrounding area, which can work against or for you depending on what you need.
