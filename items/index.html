Item Locations: Hozuki Pouches, Charms & Materials

Not every collectible is just a number on a checklist — Hozuki Pouches and Charms directly change how Musashi survives a fight. Here's what they do and how to prioritize finding them.

This guide reflects what's known shortly after launch (September 4, 2026), cross-checked across multiple independent community sources. For the full 274-item checklist across all 11 collectible categories, see our collectibles guide — this page focuses specifically on the items worth prioritizing.

Hozuki Pouches — your healing capacity

9 total, 1 by default

Hozuki Pouches directly increase how many healing charges you carry. You start with one and there are 8 more to find and upgrade — maxing out all 9 is a meaningful survivability jump, not a minor bonus, and pairs directly with the healing tools covered in our combat guide.

Charms — equippable combat bonuses

15 total

Charms are trinkets you equip for passive combat effects — things like improved healing effectiveness or reduced incoming damage. They only become enhanceable after completing the mission "The Stage Is Set at Kiyomizu," so don't worry if upgrading them isn't available yet early on.

Fine Silk Cloths & Tamahagane — enhancement materials

MaterialCountUsed for
Fine Silk Cloths25Clothing and general equipment upgrades
Tamahagane25Katana and Gauntlet enhancement — see our weapon guide and skill guide

Both are limited resources rather than something you'll naturally have a surplus of, so spend them on upgrades you're actually using — the katana tree and Gauntlet rank both feed back into combat directly, so leaning on those first tends to outperform spreading materials thin across cosmetic options.

Practical takeaway: prioritize Hozuki Pouches early since healing capacity compounds every fight afterward, pick up Charms opportunistically until "The Stage Is Set at Kiyomizu" unlocks their upgrades, and commit Fine Silk Cloths and Tamahagane to your katana and Gauntlet before spending them on anything cosmetic.

Frequently asked questions

What do Hozuki Pouches do?
Increase your healing charge capacity — 9 total, starting with 1.

What are Charms for?
Equippable trinkets with passive combat bonuses; 15 total, enhanceable after "The Stage Is Set at Kiyomizu."

What's the difference between Fine Silk Cloths and Tamahagane?
Both are enhancement materials (25 each), feeding clothing/equipment upgrades versus katana/Gauntlet upgrades respectively.
