Trophy Guide & Platinum Roadmap

52 trophies, nothing missable, and a Platinum that boils down to two clean playthroughs. Here's the breakdown and the fastest safe route through it.

This guide reflects what's known shortly after launch (September 4, 2026), cross-checked across multiple independent trophy-tracking sites. Two of these sources agree on every number below (trophy counts, hours, roadmap steps) — the one exception is the name of the hardest difficulty: most sources call it Carnage (confirmed by an actual trophy description quoting "Complete the game on difficulty level 'Carnage'"), but one guide we checked calls it Massacre instead. We're going with Carnage since it's backed by a direct trophy-text quote rather than a paraphrase, but flagging the discrepancy rather than hiding it.

Nothing is missable

Two independent trophy guides agree: there are no permanently missable trophies. If you finish the story before mopping up every collectible, choosing Continue from the main menu reportedly drops you back to right before the Chapter 7 finale, "The Clash at Rendaino" — see our quest walkthrough for that point of no return. Anything you skipped is still there to grab.

The remaining Bronze trophies mostly track story progress (one per chapter), individual boss kills, and combat milestones (Oni Armament unlocks, Oni Awakening, Issen counts) — see our combat guide, weapon guide, and skill guide for those systems in depth.

Practical takeaway: don't split your first playthrough between Story and Action — pick Action from the start so it actually counts as practice for Carnage. Chase every collectible category during that first run using our collectibles and item locations guides, since nothing locks you out early. Save Carnage and the Boss Rematches for a dedicated New Game+ cleanup run.

Frequently asked questions

How many trophies are there?
52 total — 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 9 Silver, 40 Bronze.

Are any trophies missable?
Reportedly no — Continue after the credits returns you to just before the final mission.

How long does the Platinum take?
Roughly 60-70 hours across two playthroughs. See our how long to beat guide for the full time breakdown.
