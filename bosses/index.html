Boss Guides: All Bosses & Strategies

Almost every boss in the game runs on the same underlying rhythm — this page covers that shared loop once, then breaks down what's actually different about each of the 18 fights.

Spoilers ahead. This names every boss in the game, including the final fight.
This guide reflects what's known shortly after launch (September 4, 2026), cross-checked across multiple independent community sources. Some fights below are summarized from limited reporting — treat mechanic descriptions as a strong starting read rather than a frame-perfect breakdown.

The shared boss loop

Bosses die to stamina pressure, not raw damage. This builds directly on our combat guide:

  • White-glowing attacks can't be countered with Issen at all — Parry or Deflect them instead.
  • Blue-tinted attacks call for whatever specific counter is displayed on screen at that moment.
  • Deflect is your main tool for draining a boss's stamina bar specifically.
  • Once stamina hits zero, a Break Issen prompt appears — choose maximum damage or a Soul-reward option.

Two worth calling out

Shuten Doji's regeneration

His health recovers as the fight goes on, which makes passive, cautious play actively counterproductive — staying aggressive and stacking damage faster than he heals is the actual strategy, not the usual "play it safe" instinct.

Rasho-gan's tether

This is the clearest example of the bow's tether-cutting role outside general combat — use Oni Vision to spot the cord, then cut it to shut down one of his dangerous patterns before engaging normally.

Practical takeaway: walk into every new boss defaulting to Deflect until you've learned its stamina-drain windows, and treat any white-glowing attack as an automatic "don't Issen this" regardless of how confident you feel — the loop is consistent enough that pattern recognition matters more than reflexes here.

Frequently asked questions

How many bosses are there?
18 main encounters, including enhanced and paired variants of some fights.

What do white-glowing attacks mean?
They can't be Issen'd — Parry or Deflect them instead. Blue-tinted attacks call for the specific counter shown on screen.

Who's the final boss?
Minamoto no Yoshitsune, across three phases — reported as the hardest fight in the game, with phase three requiring Oni Awakening to damage.
