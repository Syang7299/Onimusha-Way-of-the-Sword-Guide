About this site: Onimusha: Way of the Sword is an action-adventure game developed and published by Capcom, released September 4, 2026 — the first mainline entry in the Onimusha series since Onimusha: Dawn of Dreams (2006). Set in a stylized, demon-twisted version of feudal Kyoto, it follows Miyamoto Musashi (modeled on legendary actor Toshiro Mifune) as he cuts through the corrupting force known as Malice. This is an independent fan wiki and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Capcom. Guides are being written and expanded as we play through the game — check back for updates.
