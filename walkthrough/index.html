Onimusha: Way of the Sword · Walkthrough

Full Chapter Walkthrough

Seven chapters, 21 missions, spanning Kyoto's districts and the mountains beyond. Here's the shape of the whole campaign — where each Oni Armament unlocks, where each boss sits, and the one choice that ends your ability to freely explore.

Heavy spoilers ahead. This covers every chapter in order, including the final boss and the point of no return.
This guide reflects what's known shortly after launch (September 4, 2026), cross-checked across multiple independent community sources. Exact mission names within each chapter are still being confirmed as we play through ourselves — treat the chapter-level structure as solid and individual mission titles as our best current read.

The seven chapters at a glance

Seven chapters, then a hard stop before the finale

Chapter 1 — Kiyomizu-dera & the Oni Refuge

Tutorial and opening territory

Introduces core combat

The opening chapter teaches Musashi's fundamentals — see our combat guide for the full Parry/Deflect/Issen breakdown taught here.

Unlocks: The Two Celestials (paired swords) and the Oni Bow, both required story pickups — see our weapon guide.

Bosses: Sasaki Ganryu (duel), Daidara (Oni Refuge) — see our boss guides.

Chapter 2 — The Kennin-ji Whirlwind

Rakuto district, prison-window sealing

Progresses through Rakuto sealing the demonic "prison windows" and clearing miasma sources that spawn enemies as you go.

Unlocks: Wind-Whipper, from the mission "The Oni's Apprentice."

Bosses: Rasho-gan, Byakue, Ifuu.

Chapter 3 — Nijo-jo's Cruel Tale

Castle investigation and the underground laboratory

Centers on Nijo-jo Castle and the lab beneath it. Includes the mask puzzle tied to "What Lurks in the Shadows" — full solution in our puzzle solutions.

Unlocks: Earth-Shakers (great hammer), from Underground Laboratory Level 4.

Bosses: Nue (paired serpent fight), Greater Nue.

Chapter 4 — Kennin-ji, the Ten-Thousand Thunderclaps

Toribeno-focused district missions

Spreads across multiple district missions centered on the Toribeno area.

Unlocks: Firebird Flute, from mission "Shadows Fall" at Gojo Street–East.

Bosses: Dohatsu-ten, Benkei (first encounter).

Chapter 5 — Mount Oe

Mountain ascetic pursuit and cave exploration

Takes the fight up Mount Oe, including rematches against earlier bosses before a major endurance fight.

Rematches: Byakue, Dohatsu-ten, Sasaki Ganryu (second encounter).

Unlocks: Flashing Void (piercing spear), from a cave in the Castle Courtyard area.

Boss: Shuten Doji — a multi-phase endurance fight built around his health regeneration, covered in our boss guides.

Chapter 6 — Arashiyama

Western district and swamp crossing

Western Kyoto sealing missions culminating in a swamp crossing. Includes "Storm Chaser," the combined Ifuu-and-Burai encounter.

Rematch: Sasaki Ganryu (third encounter).

Unlocks: Still Water (katana), from the northeast corner of the Arashiyama swamp.

Boss: Dokyo — a chase-and-gimmick fight built around distance and teleporting copies.

Chapter 7 — The Clash at Rendaino (Finale)

Point of no return

Everything else first

Speaking to Ono no Takamura triggers the final mission and locks out free-roaming in Rakuto. Before doing this, wrap up anything you still want to finish — reporting suggests nothing is permanently missable before this point, but it's still the natural line in the sand.

Gauntlet: Rematches against Benkei, Daidara, Byakue, the Nue pair, Greater Nue, and Dohatsu-ten precede the finale.

Final Boss: Minamoto no Yoshitsune, across three phases — the hardest fight in the game, with the third phase requiring Oni Awakening to damage at all. See our boss guides and skill guide.

Practical takeaway: once you've cleared everything you want in Chapter 6, take stock before talking to Ono no Takamura — that's your actual deadline, not the chapter transitions before it. Reporting also suggests Continue after the credits drops you back to just before this mission, so a completed run doesn't permanently lock you out of finishing collectibles afterward either.

Frequently asked questions

How many chapters and missions are there?
Seven chapters across 21 missions total.

What's the point of no return?
Speaking to Ono no Takamura to start "The Clash at Rendaino" in Chapter 7 — it locks free-roaming in Rakuto.

Can you miss content permanently before that?
Reportedly no — collectibles and side content stay available until the final mission triggers.
