Characters Guide

Musashi, the people around him, and the rival who keeps crossing his path. Here's the cast and how each of them fits into the story.

This guide reflects what's known shortly after launch (September 4, 2026), cross-checked across multiple independent sources. It covers named story characters only — for the demonic Genma enemies and full boss roster, see our boss guides instead of duplicating that list here.

Miyamoto Musashi

Miyamoto Musashi

Protagonist

Modeled on legendary actor Toshiro Mifune, Musashi arrives in early Edo-period Kyoto seeking to master the "Way of the Sword." He fights with a katana that shifts between two stances, plus the paired-sword Two Celestials among his Oni Armaments — not a permanent dual-wield style, despite how some early previews described him.

Over the story he gains an Oni Gauntlet, unlocking the Parry/Deflect/Issen combat system and the skill trees that come with it.

Allies

Oni Lady

Gauntlet Spirit

A mysterious presence inhabiting Musashi's Oni Gauntlet. She serves as his guide, helping him understand what the gauntlet can do as his powers grow.

Ono no Takamura

Veteran Gauntlet-Wielder

An older, formidable warrior who carries his own Oni Gauntlet and has deep knowledge of what they are. He acts as a mentor figure — and he's the character you speak to in Chapter 7 to trigger the final mission and the point of no return.

Izumo no Okuni

Kabuki Dancer

A famous Kabuki performer seeking strength to fight the Genma herself, training under Takamura. She offers a perspective on the conflict outside the warrior class.

Rivals

Sasaki Ganryu

Rival Swordsman

Musashi's central rival — a technically gifted swordsman whose duel with Musashi is personal, separate from the Genma threat. Ganryu is also one of the 18 boss encounters, fought more than once across the campaign.

Man in White

Unknown Motives

A mysterious figure reported to have handed Oni Gauntlets to both Musashi and Ganryu. His reasons for doing so — and who he actually is — are left unclear through most of the story. This character is less consistently covered across early sources than the others on this page, so treat the details as a starting read rather than confirmed.

Practical takeaway: the cast is small and tight rather than sprawling — five named characters carry almost the entire story. If you want the mechanical side of any of them (Ganryu's boss fight, Musashi's own combat kit), our boss guides and combat guide cover that in depth; this page is the who's-who, not the how-to-fight-them.

Frequently asked questions

Who is the protagonist?
Miyamoto Musashi, modeled on Toshiro Mifune.

Who gives Musashi his Oni Gauntlet?
A mysterious figure known as the Man in White, who reportedly also armed Sasaki Ganryu.

Is Sasaki Ganryu a boss fight?
Yes — he's both a named character and a recurring boss. See our boss guides.
