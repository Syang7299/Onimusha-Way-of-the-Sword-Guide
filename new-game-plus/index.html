New Game Plus Guide

One requirement to unlock it, one currency you're about to lose, and a carryover list that's more selective than you'd expect. Here's exactly what New Game Plus and Carnage difficulty do.

This guide reflects what's known shortly after launch (September 4, 2026), cross-checked across multiple independent sources. Two detailed breakdowns agree closely on the lists below. The one point they don't fully agree on is outfits/clothing — one source marks it "unconfirmed" and another contradicts itself internally (listing outfits under both "carries over" and "resets" in the same breakdown). We're not going to guess which is right, so treat outfit carryover as genuinely unsettled until more players confirm it.

How to unlock it

Finish the story once. That's the entire requirement — no specific ending, side-content completion, or difficulty choice needed. As soon as the credits roll, both New Game Plus and Carnage difficulty (the hardest tier, above Story and Action) become available. See our trophy guide for the full Story/Action/Carnage breakdown and why Carnage matters for the Platinum.

What carries over vs. what resets

Carries Over

  • Skills — enhanced Basic and Special skills, including Oni Vision
  • Awakened Oni abilities — Eye, Arm, and Leg
  • Charms and their enhancement levels
  • Hozuki Pouches and their enhancement levels
  • Oni Armaments — stay unlocked (their upgrade level does not, see right)
  • Items and offerings
  • Genma Notes collected
  • Lion Dogs rescued
  • Boss Rematch unlock/completion status

Resets

  • Red Souls — back to zero, spend them all first
  • Sword and Gauntlet enhancement levels
  • Equipment materials — Fine Silk Cloth, Tamahagane must be recollected
  • Oni Bow — back to base level
  • Mysteries of Kyoto progress
  • Chance Encounters progress
  • Spirit Mirror unlock/access progress
  • Story and side-mission progress

The Oni Armaments themselves are worth calling out specifically: you keep having unlocked all seven going into New Game Plus (see our weapon guide), but their individual enhancement levels reset along with the rest of your gear. You'll be re-upgrading them, just without having to re-find them.

Before you start

  • Spend every Red Soul you're holding. They don't carry over, and there's no reason to bank them going into the final mission.
  • Finish collecting anything you care about first. Genma Notes, Lion Dogs, and Chance Encounters carry over by completion count where noted above, but Spirit Mirror and Mysteries of Kyoto progress do not — see our collectibles guide for what's worth prioritizing.
  • Carnage is a commitment. Once you select it for a New Game Plus run, you can't casually drop back to an easier difficulty mid-run the way you can switch between Story and Action.

Practical takeaway: New Game Plus on Carnage is really an "upgrade your character again, faster" run more than a "start from nothing" one — your skills, charms, pouches, and collectible progress mean you're not starting cold, but your sword and Gauntlet power level does reset, so don't expect to walk into Carnage overpowered. Pair this with our trophy guide's platinum roadmap if that's the goal.

Frequently asked questions

How do you unlock New Game Plus?
Finish the story once — no other requirements.

Do Red Souls carry over?
No — they reset to zero, so spend them before the final mission.

Do sword and Gauntlet upgrades carry over?
No — enhancement levels reset, though the Oni Armaments stay unlocked.
