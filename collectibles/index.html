Where to actually look

  • Get Oni Vision early. It highlights breakables and reveals treasures across the map, and it's the single biggest quality-of-life upgrade for collectible-hunting — see our skill guide.
  • Genma Notes need deliberate attention. Since they're not marked on the map like everything else, they're the category most likely to get missed on a casual playthrough.
  • Ability-gated secrets hide a chunk of this list. Our secret areas guide covers the backtracking habit that opens up routes you couldn't reach earlier.
  • Nothing is on a clock. Reporting suggests collectibles stay available right up until the Chapter 7 point of no return — see our quest walkthrough — so there's no need to 100% a chapter before moving on.

Practical takeaway: don't try to clean out every category chapter by chapter — grab Oni Vision as early as possible, keep half an eye out for Genma Notes specifically since they won't show up on your map, and save a genuine completion sweep for after your main story clear, since nothing locks out before the final mission anyway.
