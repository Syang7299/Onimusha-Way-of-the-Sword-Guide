Combat Guide: Parry, Deflect, Issen & Blaze

Musashi's combat isn't built around dodging past attacks — it's built around answering them. Every strike is a choice between four tools, and picking the right one is what actually deals damage. Here's how the whole system fits together.

This guide reflects what's known shortly after launch (September 4, 2026), cross-checked across multiple independent community sources. Exact timing windows and numbers may be adjusted by balance patches.

The Stamina Gauge comes first

Both Musashi and every enemy run on a Stamina Gauge. Draining an enemy's stamina to zero opens Break Issen — an execution. Musashi's own stamina drains from holding Block, so treat guarding as a limited resource rather than a safe default: hold it too long and your guard breaks at the worst possible moment.

Four answers to every incoming attack

Parry vs. Deflect

These share identical timing — press Block right as the hit lands — but they're not interchangeable:

ToolInputEffect
ParryBlockLow stamina cost, builds the Blaze Gauge, opens an immediate counter window
DeflectBlock + AttackStrips much more enemy stamina than Parry, reflects arrows and ranged attacks back at the source

Lean on Parry for sustained pressure and Blaze generation; switch to Deflect when you specifically want to burn an enemy's stamina down fast, or when you're dealing with ranged attackers.

Reflex Dodge: the mandatory option

Punches, kicks, and grab-style attacks can't be parried or deflected at all — Reflex Dodge is your only answer. It also fills its own Reflex Gauge; once full, your next successful Reflex Dodge slows time and opens a Reflex Combo window.

Issen: the signature counter

Issen

Start your own attack just before the enemy's strike actually connects, and Musashi sidesteps the blow and delivers a powerful counter in one motion. Miss the timing and you eat the full hit with no recovery — it's the highest-risk, highest-reward tool in the kit.

Land it against a grouped enemy and a visual cue lets you chain into the next target, and further into the one after that. The window becomes noticeably more lenient during Oni Awakening — see our skill guide for how that state is triggered and what else it does.

This is the short version — for the exact white-flash timing cue, the safer Deflect Issen variant, and what's actually confirmed about chain limits, see our Issen deep dive.

Break Issen, Blade Lock & Blade Barrage

Break Issen

Once an enemy's stamina hits zero, Break Issen becomes available. Against standard enemies it's a straightforward execution. Against bosses, it opens specific execution targets — you choose between maximizing damage or prioritizing Soul rewards.

Blade Lock

Triggers off a successful Parry or Deflect. Win the follow-up rapid-input exchange to throw the enemy off balance and strip a large chunk of stamina.

Blade Barrage

Happens when your attack and an enemy's weapon strike land at the exact same instant — switching attack type mid-clash is how you win it.

The Blaze Gauge

Stack successful Parries and the Blaze Gauge fills. Once full, Musashi enters Blazing State: increased health and stamina-damage output, and faster Soul generation on top. Since Parry is also the cheapest defensive option on your own stamina, leaning into it early in a fight is what sets up Blazing State for the rest of it.

Souls and upgrade materials

Souls are the currency generated through combat, and Blazing State increases how fast you earn them. Rather than only grinding for upgrade drops, you can spend Red Souls directly at Yanimasa's Monument for materials like Tempered Iron, Whetstone, Malicite, and Leather — worth checking before you go hunting a specific material the hard way. Blue Souls specifically feed the Oni Power Gauge that lets you activate an Oni Armament — see our weapon guide for what each one does.

Watch for boss soul absorption

Some bosses punish getting hit directly by absorbing your Souls and powering into a more aggressive second phase off the damage. Their unblockable, pink-tinted attacks are the ones to treat as must-avoid rather than something to trade through — see our boss guides for fight-specific detail as we cover them.

Practical takeaway: default to Parry for general pressure and Blaze building, switch to Deflect against ranged attackers or when you want to burn stamina fast, treat Reflex Dodge as mandatory rather than optional against grabs and strikes, and only reach for Issen once you can reliably read a specific enemy's tell — a missed Issen costs far more than a missed Parry.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between Parry and Deflect?
Same timing, different effect — Parry costs less stamina and builds Blaze, Deflect strips more enemy stamina and reflects projectiles.

When should I use Reflex Dodge instead of Parry?
Against punches, kicks, and grabs — these can't be parried at all, so Reflex Dodge is mandatory rather than a choice.

What happens if a boss hits me?
Some bosses absorb your Souls off a successful hit and power into a more aggressive phase — their pink-tinted unblockable attacks are the priority to avoid.
