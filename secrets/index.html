Secret Areas: Hidden Routes, Locked Doors & Oni Shortcuts

A lot of what looks like a dead end on your first pass through Kyoto isn't one — it's just waiting on an ability you don't have yet. Here's how to actually read the environment instead of guessing.

This guide reflects what's known shortly after launch (September 4, 2026), cross-checked across multiple independent community sources. This covers the general techniques for finding secret areas rather than a room-by-room list — see our collectibles guide for the itemized checklist these areas often feed into.

Read the environment first

Blocked passages, unusual wall textures, elevated platforms you can't obviously reach, and gaps that seem too convenient are all signals that a route exists nearby — the game telegraphs secrets rather than hiding them arbitrarily. Turn on Oni Vision (see our skill guide) whenever you're exploring seriously; it reveals hidden objects and environmental elements that aren't visible otherwise.

Ability-gated backtracking

Several secrets are locked behind Oni abilities Musashi hasn't unlocked yet — most notably Oni Strength and Oni Agility. That means the correct habit isn't "explore every area to 100% the first time you're there" — it's "note what looked blocked, then come back after your next major ability unlock."

Most missed secrets are a backtracking problem, not a searching problem

Locked doors with no visible switch

The bow-and-window trick

Some locked doors genuinely can't be opened from the front — there's no switch to find on that side. Instead, make your way to the back of the room, where you'll usually find a broken window opening. Equip the bow, aim through the window at the unlock mechanism on the far side of the door, and fire. That's what actually opens it.

Oni Shortcuts

Oni Glyphs → portals

With Oni Vision active, keep an eye out for Oni Glyphs — interact with one and Musashi channels Oni Power to turn it into a short-range portal you can walk straight through. These function as one-time or reusable shortcuts rather than full-blown secret rooms, but they're an easy way to cut travel time once you know to look for them.

Practical takeaway: don't chase 100% completion on your first pass through any district — mark what looked interesting but unreachable, keep playing, and sweep back through with Oni Strength and Oni Agility once you have them. You'll save yourself from replaying areas you already technically visited.

Frequently asked questions

Why can't I reach some areas on my first visit?
Many routes are gated behind Oni abilities like Strength or Agility that you unlock later — revisit after major story milestones.

How do I open a locked door with no switch?
Find the broken window on the far side of the room and shoot the unlock mechanism through it with the bow.

What are Oni Shortcuts?
Portals created by interacting with Oni Glyphs (revealed via Oni Vision) — a fast way to cut through terrain you'd otherwise have to walk around.
